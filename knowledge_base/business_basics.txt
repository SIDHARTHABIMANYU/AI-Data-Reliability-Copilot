Business Basics

A business creates value by solving a problem for a specific group of customers.

Common business models include subscription, marketplace, transaction-based, advertising, licensing, and direct sales.

A subscription business charges customers on a recurring basis for continued access to a product or service.

A marketplace connects buyers and sellers and typically earns revenue through commissions or transaction fees.

Customer segmentation divides a market into groups based on characteristics such as needs, behavior, demographics, or purchasing patterns.

A business idea should be evaluated based on the customer problem, target market, value proposition, business model, competition, costs, and potential risks.
