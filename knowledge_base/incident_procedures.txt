Retail Incident Procedures

Incident Investigation

When an incident is created:

1. Identify the affected dataset and file.
2. Identify the incident type and severity.
3. Review the latest pipeline run.
4. Review data quality results.
5. Review anomaly detection results when applicable.
6. Review upstream and downstream lineage.
7. Identify affected business metrics.
8. Determine the most likely root cause.
9. Record supporting evidence.
10. Recommend the safest next action.

Incident Severity

HIGH:
The incident significantly affects critical business reporting or production data.

MEDIUM:
The incident affects a business dataset or metric but does not indicate immediate critical impact.

LOW:
The incident has limited impact and does not significantly affect business reporting.

Evidence Requirements

AI recommendations should be based on available evidence.

Evidence may include:

- Pipeline run status
- Row counts
- Schema validation results
- Data quality results
- Anomaly scores
- Historical baselines
- Dataset contracts
- Pipeline runbooks
- Data lineage
- Business metric definitions

The AI must clearly identify when evidence is insufficient to determine a root cause.
