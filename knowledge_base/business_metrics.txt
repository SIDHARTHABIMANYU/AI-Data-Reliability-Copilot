Retail Business Metrics

Daily Revenue

Daily revenue is calculated from the sales dataset by aggregating sales_amount by transaction date and store.

Transaction Count

Transaction count represents the number of sales transactions recorded for a store and date.

Sales Quantity

Sales quantity represents the total quantity of products sold.

Stock Availability

Stock availability is derived from inventory records and indicates whether products are available for sale.

Supplier Performance

Supplier performance is derived from supplier delivery records and measures delivered quantities and supplier-related operational metrics.

A failure in the sales pipeline can affect daily revenue, transaction count, and sales quantity dashboards.

A failure in the inventory pipeline can affect stock availability reporting.

A failure in the supplier pipeline can affect supplier performance reporting.
