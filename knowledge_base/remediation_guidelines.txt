Retail Remediation Guidelines

General Rule

AI-generated remediation suggestions must be treated as recommendations.

The AI must not assume that a proposed change is safe without supporting evidence.

Schema Change

If a column is renamed:

1. Compare old and new schemas.
2. Inspect sample values.
3. Compare business definitions.
4. Check downstream transformations.
5. Propose a mapping only when the evidence supports equivalence.
6. Validate the proposed transformation before production use.

Duplicate Data

For duplicate transactions:

1. Identify duplicate transaction identifiers.
2. Determine whether duplicates represent repeated ingestion.
3. Confirm the dataset contract.
4. Remove duplicates according to the defined business rule.
5. Validate the resulting row count.

Missing Data

For missing files:

1. Confirm the expected file.
2. Check the latest successful ingestion.
3. Check upstream availability.
4. Determine affected downstream metrics.
5. Do not fabricate missing data.
6. Escalate when the source cannot be recovered.

Anomaly

For anomalous business metrics:

1. Verify the anomaly using statistical evidence.
2. Compare against historical behavior.
3. Check for related pipeline or data-quality failures.
4. Determine whether the anomaly is a genuine business event or a data issue.
5. Avoid modifying data solely because an anomaly was detected.
