Retail Data Contracts

Sales Dataset

Required columns:
transaction_id
transaction_date
store_id
product_id
quantity
sales_amount

Business meaning:
sales_amount represents the recorded sales value of a transaction.

Inventory Dataset

Required columns:
inventory_date
store_id
product_id
stock_quantity

Business meaning:
stock_quantity represents the available inventory quantity for a store and product on a given date.

Supplier Dataset

Required columns:
supplier_id
supplier_name
product_id
delivery_date
quantity_delivered

Business meaning:
quantity_delivered represents the quantity delivered by a supplier.

Schema changes must be investigated before automatically modifying downstream transformations.

A renamed column must not automatically be assumed to have the same business meaning as the previous column.
